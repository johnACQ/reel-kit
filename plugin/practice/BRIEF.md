## What goes on screen
"Paste this prompt here" means this prompt, word for word, on a prompt card:

```
Interview me about my business so you can help me run it.
Ask ONE question at a time and wait for my answer.
Cover: what I do and don't do, where I work, who my best customers are,
what a typical customer buys and what it costs, how people find me, how I price and quote,
what I hate doing, and how I talk to customers.
After about 20 questions, write a one-page summary of my business
that I can paste into a project's instructions.
```

"How to talk, what to do, what not to do" is a house rules list. These are the real lines:
- Who we are
- What we do, and what we don't
- Where we work
- How we talk
- Never say
- Always: draft, don't send
