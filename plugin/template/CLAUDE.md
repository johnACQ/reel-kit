# My reels

This folder is where Claude edits my reels with HyperFrames and reel-kit.

- Before every edit, read EDITING-STYLE.md, frame.md and brand.json in this folder.
- One folder per reel. Never change or delete my original footage.
- Nothing renders until I OK the beat sheet.
- Real footage only. No fake screenshots, reviews or numbers. Every number on screen has a source.
- Captions say what I said. Never change a number or a "not".
- When I say a note is for every reel, save it to EDITING-STYLE.md with the date.
