# Filming card

Read this before you hit record. The way you film decides which effects you get.

## Before you roll
1. Phone on a tripod. Back camera, 4K, vertical. The shot never moves.
2. Waist up, with the top of your head about a third of the way down the frame. That's the room for cards beside you and words behind your head. A tight selfie with your forehead at the top leaves nowhere for the effects to go.
3. Light in front of you, on your face. Never a window behind you.
4. Stand a couple of metres off the wall. A clean gap behind you gives a clean cut-out.
5. Quiet room, mic close.
6. Film a 5-second test first and ask Claude to check it: "check my test shot, test.mov".

## While you talk
- Say the effects out loud, then pause a beat: "zoom in on my hand", "put the three steps up here". Claude finds each one on the exact word.
- Say the count first ("three things") if you want a list effect.
- Hold still for a second where an effect lands.
- Bad takes are fine. Keep rolling and say the line again.

## Before you stop
- Step out of frame and film 2 seconds of the empty room. Claude uses it as a clean background for cut-out effects.

## Then
Make a folder for the reel, drop the take in, and say "edit take.mov". The first thing back is a beat sheet to OK, not a video.
