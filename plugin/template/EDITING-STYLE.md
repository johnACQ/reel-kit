# My editing style

Claude reads this before every edit. Every line here applies to every reel unless it says otherwise.

## Starting defaults
- Vertical 1080 x 1920. Keep the frame rate of the footage.
- Captions in the bottom third (y 1280 to 1470), three or four words at a time, the word I'm saying highlighted. Never over my face. If my chin drops into the caption band, reframe the shot lower instead of moving the text up.
- Cards float in the top half, beside or above my head. One accent colour, always as a fill with dark text on it.
- A new visual about every 4 seconds. Never the same kind twice in a row. At most one zoom every 5 seconds. My best line stays clean.
- Cut the pauses, false starts and bad takes. Keep a breath at each edge. Never cut inside a word.
- Beat sheet before anything renders.
- On-screen text in plain words: short, no em dashes, nothing I didn't say or approve.
- Speech first. Music only if I have the rights to it, and quiet under my voice.
